Agentic Behavioral Certification Standard

The ARE behavioral certification standard is published at Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19169185.

The standard defines what an Agentic Behavioral Certification covers, how the hash chain is structured, and how a third party (regulator, auditor) can independently verify it without trusting ARE's attestation.
